About The Hub

The Hub doesn't store anything of its own. It reads directly from The Pause, The Keep, and The Record's data on this device — all three apps share the same origin, so this page can see their entries without any syncing or server in between.

The weekly numbers here are just counts, nothing interpreted. Nothing leaves this device unless you tap a backup or share button yourself.

The full backup bundles all three apps' data into one file. Restoring it writes each app's data back to where it belongs — The Pause's entries go to The Pause, and so on. It's all-or-nothing per app, same as restoring within each app individually.
